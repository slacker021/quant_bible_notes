# Disclaimer
### On Generative Artificial Intelligence
This knowledge base makes heavy use of generative artificial intelligence, more specifically autonomous AI agents tasked with various roles in the development of this repository. AI is used in the following ways:
- **Initial Note Generation:** The original note drafts are first generated by a dedicated high-level agent possessing advanced reasoning capabilities. Each draft is then carefully inspected by the author to check for any errors or violations of conventions.  
- **Proofs and Derivations:** Proofs and derivations for known lemmas, propositions, and theorems are constructed by the same high-level agents. To ensure the proofs are correct, these agents verify their accuracy with [Lean 4](https://lean-lang.org/). Given that the lemmas, propositions, and theorems presented here are already known to be true with proofs established across the literature, rederiving everything manually from scratch is an inefficient use of time. Therefore, the reader will find only verified results rather than unresolved conjectures. 
- **Errors:** Both the author and lower-level agents take turns checking for errors, whether logical or grammatical. This collaborative proofreading ensures mutual accountability. 
- **Framework Consistency:** Ensuring that newly introduced modules remain consistent with previously established facts is challenging. The vast differences in notation, conventions, and assumptions across source materials make standardizing integrated information difficult. AI is therefore essential to ensure that newly incorporated material aligns seamlessly with the rest of the framework.

# Pedagogy

### Early Versus Late Transcendentals
This repository of notes follows a very different learning trajectory from most curricula presented by bootcamps and university programs. Most curricula follow the *Early Transcendental* paradigm of mathematics pedagogy and education. This method of learning is primarily top-down, with an emphasis on building intuition and applying facts learned early on. Meanwhile, mathematical rigor is treated as an afterthought; even when taught, it arrives much later in the learner's journey. Such a method can be problematic because it requires the learner to absorb knowledge rapidly through extensive memorization. While this may work for accelerated learners, others encounter difficulties and are discouraged from continuing to learn. 

Another paradigm of learning, a form of *Late Transcendental* education, takes the opposite approach: a bottom-up progression that stresses strong formalism and absolute rigor. A notable example was the *Soviet* tradition of mathematics pedagogy, characterized by depth and thoroughness over pattern-matching and rote memorization. Such a paradigm ensured that learners understood exactly what they were reading and could explain the underlying concepts. However, this approach can easily lead to cognitive overload. Additionally, instructors utilizing this framework rarely provided complete proofs for every claim, as doing so entailed substantial effort that could not be accommodated within reasonable time constraints. 

This repository aims to provide a modernized adaptation of that rigorous tradition:
- **All Proofs Shown:** All proofs are displayed in full to eliminate hidden abstractions. This enables the learner to focus on leveraging established results to prove advanced propositions, apply them to computationally intensive problems, or formulate new insights. 
- **Visual Aids:** The modules are accompanied by elegantly simple figures and visualizations to help learners picture the phenomena in question. Visual representations are often retained far more effectively by readers than purely abstract symbolic manipulations. 
- **Examples and Intuition:** Examples and intuitive explanations are provided in the form of toggleable *callouts*. These enhance the learning experience by providing concrete, accessible perspectives that complement the rigorous formal statements.

These notes do not attempt to reinvent established theory. Instead, they aggregate and organize verified mathematical facts into a coherent, unified framework. The goal is to provide the aspiring independent quant researcher with a rich and rigorous foundation in the mathematical sciences from which to formulate their own conjectures. Market regimes, regulatory frameworks, and technologies will evolve, but the foundational principles presented here are anchored in a timeless structure that transcends any particular era.

### Who is this For? 
Although this intellectual haven remains open to the general public, it is intended for a very specific type of reader: The independent quant researcher. Unlike most references within contemporary quantitative finance, this reference is an exhaustive, ever-growing, and unapologetically rigorous treatise of various relevant topics that blend together to form a coherent and beautiful social science. 

While markets will constantly evolve, much of what quantitative finance is built on is based on timeless principles established decades or even centuries ago. In theory, it should not be difficult to learn these timeless truths. However, much of said timeless knowledge has become heavily abstracted thanks to the Early Transcendental Curriculum. Thus, the independent researcher is burdened with having to consult disparate resources—with each resource using conflicting notation and assumptions—just to learn what is relevant. 

In an age dominated by generative AI, the risk of abstraction is amplified by many researchers relying on results provided by LLMs. While these LLMs are useful when utilized properly, with these LLMs having been developed so well that they provide very accurate and useful advice, these LLMs often provide information that is so abstracted and complicated that the researcher does not understand what is going on under the hood. 

This haven is the exact opposite of contemporary courses and references: A return to the basics. It focuses on the fundamental theory, going as far as to include topics that most pedagogical frameworks would consider "impractical" or "irrelevant." This repository assumes that the independent researcher operates with a mindset of unhurried confidence and a desire to explore quantitative finance with deep intellectual respect. 

### Who is this Not For? 
It is crucial that the reader be aware of the limitations of this project, as it does not attempt to cater to the needs of everyone. Below are major limitations the reader must keep in mind: 
- **No Exercise Problems:** This knowledgebase contains no exercise problems meant to be tackled. This is not meant to test one's knowledge by having them tackle exercise problems, whether proofs or computational ones. The most this repository provides are examples and applications, with both being given full explanations. The goal of this repository is to provide the non-abstracted knowledge needed to tackle complex social systems, with the information provided being very rigorous and explicit with its steps. 
- **No Interview Guides and Questions:** No advice is provided on how to tackle interview questions that are administered by the human resources team at a firm. It is up to the reader to use the knowledge provided here, along with supplementary advice provided by external sources, on practice for interview questions. This is in line with keeping this place a zone dedicated purely to intellectual inquiry and communal learning. 
- **Not For Crammers:** The exhaustive and zero-abstraction framework present in this knowledgebase requires a lot of time, effort, and energy to parse. This is not ideal for those who are attempting to quickly grasp a topic, whether because they seek guidance on immediate applications or are cramming for a test. However, this may be an excellent supplementary resource for those who feel their current learning material has severe logical leaps.
