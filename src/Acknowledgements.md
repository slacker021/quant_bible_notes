# Primary Sources
These are the sources that various volumes and their respective modules are based on. _Primary Sources_ provide not only the information but the framework upon which the topics are built on, including details such as the order of topics introduced and the pedagogy used.

### Pure and Applied Mathematics
##### Real Analysis
- **Volume 1 - Classical Elementary Analysis:** _Mathematical Analysis 1: Second Edition_ by _Vladimir Antonovich Zorich_, published by _Springer_ as part of _Universitext_
- **Volume 2 - Classical Intermediate and Advanced Calculus:** _Mathematical Analysis 2: Second Edition_ by _Vladimir Antonovich Zorich_, published by _Springer_ as part of _Universitext_
- **Volume 3 - Abstract Analysis, Measure Theory, and Integration:** _Real Analysis: Measures, Integrals, and Applications_ by _Makarov_ and _Podkoryov_, published by _Springer_ as part of _Universitext_  
    **Supplementary Literature**
    - _Calculus: Ninth Edition_ by _James Stewart_, published by _Cengage_

##### Stochastic Analysis
- **Volume 1 - Random Variables and Distributions:** _Probability Theory 1: Random Variables and Distrubutions_ by _Andrea Pascucci_, published by _Springer_ as part of _Universitext_
- **Volume 2 - Stochastic Calculus:** _Probability Theory 2: Stochastic Calculus_ by _Andrea Pascucci_, published by _Springer_ as part of _Universitext_

##### Linear Analysis
- **Volume 1 - Elementary Linear Algebra:** _Linear Algebra Done Right: Fourth Edition_ by _Sheldon Axler_, published by _Springer_ as part of _Undergraduate Texts in Mathematics_

##### Numerical Analysis
- **Volume 1 - Elementary Numerical Analysis:** _Theoretical Numerical Analysis: A Functional Analysis Framework (Third Edition)_ by _Atkinson_ and _Han_, published by _Springer_ as part of _Texts in Applied Mathematics_  
    **Supplementary Literature**
    - _Numerical Analysis: Third Edition_ by _Timothy Sauer_, published by _Pearson_
    - _Numerical Recipes: The Art of Scientific Computing (Third Edition)_, published by _Cambridge University Press_

##### Partial Differential Equations
- **Volume 1 - Basic Theory:** _Partial Differential Equations 1: Basic Theory (Third Edition)_ by _Michael E. Taylor_, published by Springer as part of _Applied Mathematics Series_
- **Volume 2 - Linear Equations:** _Partial Differential Equations 2: Qualitative Studies of Linear Equations (Third Edition)_ by _Michael E. Taylor_, published by Springer as part of _Applied Mathematics Series_
- **Volume 3 - Nonlinear Equations:** _Partial Differential Equations 3: Nonlinear Equations (Third Edition)_ by _Michael E. Taylor_, published by Springer as part of _Applied Mathematics Series_  
    **Supplementary Literature**
    - _Handbook of Linear Partial Differential Equations for Engineers and Scientists: Second Edition_ by _Polyanin_ and _Nazaikinskii_, published by _CRC Press_ as part of _Handbooks of Mathematical Equations_
    - _Handbook of Nonlinear Partial Differential Equations: Second Edition_ by _Polyanin_ and _Zaitsev_, published by _CRC Press_ as part of _Handbooks of Mathematical Equations_
    - _Handbook of Integral Equations: Second Edition_ by _Polyanin_ and _Manzhirov_, published by _CRC Press_ as part of _Handbooks of Mathematical Equations_

##### Dynamical Systems
- **Volume 1 - Ordinary Differential Equations:** _Dynamical Systems 1: Ordinary Differential Equations and Smooth Dynamical Systems_ edited by _D.V. Anosov_ and _V.I. Arnold_, published by _Springer_ as part of _Encyclopedia of Mathematical Sciences: Volume 1_
- **Volume 2 - Ergodic Theory:** _Dynamical Systems 2: Ergodic Theory with Applications to Dynamical Systems and Statistical Mechanics_ edited by _Ya. G. Sinai_, published by _Springer_ as part of _Encyclopedia of Mathematical Sciences: Volume 2_
- **Volume 3 - Classical and Celestial Mechanics:** _Dynamical Systems 3: Mathematical Aspects of Classical and Celestial Mechanics_, edited by _Arnold, Kozlov, and Neishtadt_, published by _Springer_ as part of _Encyclopedia of Mathematical Sciences: Volume 3_

### Computer Science

##### Data Structures and Algorithms
- **Volume 1 - Foundational Computer Science:** _Introduction to Algorithms: Fourth Edition_ by _Cormen, Leierson, Rivest_, and _Stein_, published by _Massachusetts Institute of Technology Press_
- **Volume 2 - Algorithm Complexity:** _Beyond the Worst-Case Analysis of Algorithms_ edited by _Tim Roughgarden_, published by _Cambridge University Press_  
    **Supplementary Literature**
    - _The Art of Computer Programming: Volume 1, Fundamental Algorithms (Third Edition)_ by _Donald E. Knuth_, published by _Addison-Weasley_

##### Julia Programming
- **Design Patterns:** _Hands-On Design Patterns and Best Practices with Julia: Proven Solutions to Common Problems in Software Design for Julia 1.x_, published by _Packt_

### Finance and Economics
##### Portfolio Management
- **Volume 1 - Modern Portfolio Theory:** _Modern Portfolio Theory and Investment Analysis: Ninth Edition_ by _Elton, Gruber, Brown_, and _Goetzmann_, published by _John Wiley & Sons_  
    **Supplementary Literature**
    - _Stochastic Finance: A Numeraire Approach_ by _Jan Vercer_, published by _Chapman & Hall_ under _CRC Press_ as part of _Financial Mathematics Series_

### Statistics
- **Volume 1 - Elementary Mathematical Statistics:** _Mathematical Statistics, Basic Ideas and Selected Topics: Volume 1_, published by _CRC Press_ as part of _Texts in Statistical Science_
- **Volume 2 - Intermediate and Advanced Mathematical Statistics:** _Mathematical Statistics, Basic Ideas and Selected Topics: Volume 2_, published by _CRC Press_ as part of _Texts in Statistical Science_
