# Primary Sources
These are the sources that various volumes and their respective modules are based on. *Primary Sources* provide not only the information but the manner and style of education that the notes build upon: 

### Mathematics
##### Real Analysis
- **Volume 1 - Classical Elementary Analysis:** *Mathematical Analysis 1: Second Edition* by *Vladimir Antonovich Zorich*
- **Volume 2 -  Classical Intermediate and Advanced Calculus:** *Mathematical Analysis 2: Second Edition* by *Vladimir Antonovich Zorich*
- **Volume 3 - Abstract Analysis, Measure Theory, and Integration:** *Real Analysis: Measures, Integrals, and Applications* by *Makarov* and *Podkoryov*

**Stochastic Analysis**
- **Volume 1 - Random Variables and Distributions:** *Probability Theory 1: Random Variables and Distrubutions* by *Andrea Pascucci*
- **Volume 2 - Stochastic Calculus:** *Probability Theory 2: Stochastic Calculus* by *Andrea Pascucci*

**Linear Analysis**
- **Volume 1 - Elementary Linear Algebra:** *Linear Algebra Done Right: Fourth Edition* by *Sheldon Axler*

### Computer Science 

##### Data Structures and Algorithms
- **Volume 1 - Foundational Computer Science:** *Introduction to Algorithms: Fourth Edition* by *Cormen, Leierson, Rivest*, and *Stein*

##### Julia Programming
- **Design Patterns:** *Hands-On Design Patterns and Best Practices with Julia: Proven Solutions to Common Problems in Software Design for Julia 1.x*

### Finance and Economics

##### Portfolio Management
- **Volume 1 - Modern Portfolio Theory:** *Modern Portfolio Theory and Investment Analysis: Ninth Edition* by *Elton, Gruber, Brown*, and *Goetzmann*

### Statistics
- **Volume 1 - Basic Models, Regression, and Inference:** *Mathematical Statistics, Basic Ideas and Selected Topics: Volume 1*
- **Volume 2 - Intermediate Methods and Unconventional Models:** *Mathematical Statistics, Basic Ideas and Selected Topics: Volume 2*
